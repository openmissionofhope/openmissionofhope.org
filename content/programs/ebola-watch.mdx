Ebola Watch helps humanitarian organisations, researchers, journalists and the public understand
how much Ebola has officially been reported, how much probably exists, how it may spread, and how
confident anyone can be.

**Use it:** [ebolawatch.openmissionofhope.org](https://ebolawatch.openmissionofhope.org)

## What it does

- Ingests official data (WHO, ECDC and national sources) with provenance, keeping every published version
- Watches news and other unofficial reports in many languages, kept visibly separate from official figures
- Estimates the true burden and forecasts several scenarios, with uncertainty shown and backtested
- Publishes in 13 languages, including Swahili, Lingala, Hausa, Amharic and Arabic

When the evidence is insufficient, it says so. Ebola Watch is not a medical service and holds no
personal or patient data.
