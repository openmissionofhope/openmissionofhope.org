Agape connects someone who needs practical help with a person who volunteers to give it: a meal,
groceries, a ride, a jump start, a place to stay tonight, donated items, or help arranging a bill.
Nobody pays, bids or owes anything, and Agape never holds money.

**Use it:** [agape.openmissionofhope.org](https://agape.openmissionofhope.org)

## How it works

1. Someone asks for help, with an approximate area. Their exact address stays private.
2. Signed-in volunteers nearby see the request and offer to help. Each helper spot can be taken by one person only.
3. They coordinate privately, the help happens in person, and the requester confirms it.

Reporting, blocking and moderation are built in, accounts are for adults, and everyone is welcome
whatever their faith or none.

## Emergency assistance

Agape also includes an emergency page that always shows the direct call to 911, and is being
prepared to let people ask a volunteer to call 911 for them, help with CPR nearby, or bring an AED.
Those volunteer emergency requests stay switched off until volunteer coverage and alert delivery
have been confirmed; the site says so plainly.
